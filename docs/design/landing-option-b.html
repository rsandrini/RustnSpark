2 · Specialise

Different ships, different jobs

You do not pick a class from a menu. You build toward one, and the ship takes the shape of what you fitted.

  • Hauler

    Mostly cargo holds. Built to carry loads.

  • Transport

    Pressurized cabins and life support, for passengers.

  • Warship

    Weapons and defense first.

  • Miner

    A mining rig on board, and room for the ore.

  • Multirole

    No single specialty. A bit of everything.

4 · Choose

A mission for every ship

Each job tells you what it asks of your ship before you accept. If your ship does not qualify, it says why.

  • Delivery

    Carry cargo from port to port.

    NeedsCargo space for the load.
  • Transport

    Fly passengers where they are going.

    NeedsA Passenger Cabin plus a Life Support module.
  • Escort

    Keep a client's ship alive through dangerous space.

    NeedsAt least one weapon and good mobility to keep up.
  • Mining

    Dig ore at a place, then sell it.

    NeedsA Mining Rig and cargo space for the ore.
  • Rescue

    Reach survivors and bring them home.

    NeedsA fast ship and room for the survivors.
  • Race

    Beat three to five rivals. The prize depends on where you finish.

    NeedsSpeed at or above the race's entry minimum.
  • Scavenge

    Search the wreckage around a port for used parts and scrap.

    You start itAny time, from the port.
  • Travel

    Take your ship somewhere on your own. It costs only fuel.

    You start itAny time, no job needed.

Not a game you can just leave running

  • A living economy

    Upgrades need money plus scrap, crystals and rare cores from dangerous places. Ore you dig up is yours to sell.

  • Everything costs

    Pirates, wear and running dry. A tow costs credits and the mission. Spare parts and cargo compete for space.

  • Read every run

    Each trip is told like a text RPG: fights, wear, loot and what it cost. Repair, upgrade and go again.
