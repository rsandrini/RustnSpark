# The far places are zone 3, not a new zone

The three deep fields beyond the map are placed in zone 3 with the most dangerous routes (danger 8–10) rather than in new zones 4 and 5. The zone scale 0–3 is a database rule (a check constraint on locations), and the pirate strength, scavenging and wear tables are keyed by it; a fourth tier would have meant a migration and new rows in every one of them. The difficulty of the far places comes from distance, route environments and a rare-only mining yield instead of a higher zone number.
